Pine Ridge Chamber App
Version: 0.11

Changes in V0.11:
- All measurement entry boxes are yellow by default.
- Entry text remains red.
- Field popup now switches a box between standard yellow and lime green.
- Lime green is intended to flag an unusual or changed measurement.
- New chambers initialize every editable box as yellow.
- Existing blank/white V0.10 fields display as yellow when opened in V0.11.
